

# Telegram Animation Bridge

## Overview

Welcome to the Telegram Animation Bridge! This is a straightforward desktop application designed to help you move your favorite animated and static sticker packs from Telegram over to WhatsApp.

Because WhatsApp has very strict rules about media formats, you cannot normally share stickers between the two apps. This software automates the entire process: simply give it a Telegram link, and it will download, resize, and convert the stickers into a perfectly formatted package ready for your phone.

---

## Instructions

### ONE: Convert a Telegram Pack

1. Open Telegram and copy the share link of the sticker pack you want (e.g., `[https://t.me/addstickers/YourFavoritePack](https://t.me/addstickers/YourFavoritePack)`).
2. Open the Animation Bridge desktop application on your PC.
3. Paste the link into the text box and click **Download**.
4. The software will automatically process the files and save a WhatsApp-ready folder to your computer.

### TWO: Customizing Your Own Packs

1. If you created your own custom stickers using Telegram's official `@Stickers` bot, you can easily port them to WhatsApp too!
2. Just copy the public link to your newly created Telegram pack.
3. Follow the steps in **Hammer ONE** to download and convert your custom creations for WhatsApp.

### THREE: Moving to Your Phone

1. Once the desktop app finishes converting, transfer the output folder from your PC to your Android phone.
2. You can then use any standard sticker-importing app on your phone to instantly load these correctly formatted files into your WhatsApp account.

---

## Notes

WhatsApp enforces strict limitations on what it allows into its app. Our software automatically handles these restrictions for you:

* **Dimensions:** Every sticker is automatically processed into the exact 512x512 pixel resolution required by WhatsApp.
* **File Type:** All media is correctly transcoded into the `.webp` format.
* **Tray Icon:** The app helps ensure you have the required 96x96 pixel tray image for your WhatsApp sticker menu.
* **Quantity Limits:** Please note that WhatsApp restricts packs to a minimum of 3 and a maximum of 30 stickers per pack.
